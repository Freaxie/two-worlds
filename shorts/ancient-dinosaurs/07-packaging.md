## 2. Thumbnail concept

**Format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16); export a 2160 × 3840 master. Shorts mostly show a chosen frame in the feed, but custom thumbnails appear in search, on the channel page and in shares. Custom Shorts thumbnail upload has been rolling out on desktop; frame-picking works in the mobile app. **Also place this image as a 1-frame insert at the very end of S29**, so you can select it as the frame whether or not custom upload is available to you. *(Check YouTube Help for the current rules on your account.)*

**Image: "Torch and Skull"**

- **Lower third:** the Wanderer (hooded ancient traveler) holds up a torch. Their face is half-lit with warm orange light, and they look **up**.
- **Upper two-thirds:** an enormous **dinosaur skull with a long tooth row**, half-emerging from a dark sandstone cliff. It is real fossil texture: cracked, dark and mineralized.
- **The contrast between antiquity and prehistory:** *warm torchlight* falls on the human and the front of the skull (antiquity); *cold blue-grey moonlight* falls on the rest of the cliff (deep time). In the darkness behind the skull, **drifting dust faintly forms the ghost-silhouette of the living animal's head**, so the viewer sees what the ancient person couldn't.
- **Composition:** a strong diagonal from the torch to the skull's eye socket. The human is tiny, the fossil huge: scale contrast is the hook.
- Base image: prompt **T01** in 05-ai-prompts.md.

**Text (2–4 words), with three options:**

| Option | Text | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | **GIANT? DRAGON? NO.** | Implies the twist without lying. The video shows what they actually thought, and why it was wrong |
| B | **NOT A GIANT** | A single, sharp correction |
| C | **THEY DIDN'T KNOW** | Mystery and empathy; true to the thesis |

**Text styling:** Cormorant Garamond Bold caps, off-white `#F1E8D6`, with **"NO."** (or the key word) in gold `#C9A45C`. Place it in the upper 20% of the frame over the darkest sky. Stroke-free, with a soft dark glow for contrast. Text height is about 9% of the frame, so it stays readable at feed size.

**Avoid:** red arrows, circles, shocked faces, a cartoon T. rex, any implication of a "hidden truth", and generated "ancient artwork" showing dinosaurs.

## 4. Pinned comment (for engagement and sources)

> **Two bonus facts that didn't fit in 90 seconds:**
>
> 🦣 Ancient people *did* draw one extinct giant: Ice Age artists painted and engraved **mammoths** on cave walls (Rouffignac in France is nicknamed the "Cave of the Hundred Mammoths"), because they saw them alive. The last mammoths survived on Wrangel Island until about 4,000 years ago, *after* the Great Pyramid was built. Nobody ever saw a living non-avian dinosaur.
>
> 🐦 Technically, ancient people wrote about dinosaurs constantly. **Birds are living dinosaurs.** Every Roman pigeon counts.
>
> **Sources:** Meier et al. 2024 (*Sci. Rep.*, Mycenae fossil); Suetonius, *Augustus* 72; Plot 1677; Owen 1842; Cuvier 1796; Witton & Hing 2024 (*Interdisciplinary Science Reviews*, on why the griffin-Protoceratops idea doesn't hold up); Adrienne Mayor, *The First Fossil Hunters*.
