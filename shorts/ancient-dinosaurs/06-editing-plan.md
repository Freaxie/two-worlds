**Cutting principles**
- **Investigation (0:16–0:45):** cut on the stressed word or the musical hit, never mid-word. Average shot about 2.3 s.
- **Reveal (0:45–1:14):** let shots breathe. The **S19 silence** is the most important edit in the film. Don't fill it.
- **Ending:** S27 is the longest shot in the film on purpose.
- **Loop:** the last frame's texture and wind audio must match S01, so a rewatch feels continuous. This raises Shorts replay rate.

## 2. Music

Use licensed library music (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed or similar) or a commissioned score. **No vocals with lyrics, and no trailer "braams".**

| Cue | Time | Brief | Library search terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 "Undercurrent"** | 0:00–0:16 | Sub-bass drone and airy textures; one low piano note on the question; a soft heartbeat pulse (about 70 BPM) from 0:08 | "dark documentary ambient", "mystery drone underscore", "archaeology atmosphere" |
| **M2 "Evidence"** | 0:16–0:45 | A low-string staccato ostinato, frame drum and muted metallic ticks; builds gently; accents on each location cut. Feel: investigative, not action | "investigation pulse strings documentary", "cinematic tension ostinato minimal" |
| **M3 "Silence"** | 0:45–0:47 | Full drop. Room tone only | n/a |
| **M4 "Revelation"** | 0:47–1:15 | Warm sustained low strings with a slow harmonic rise; a dissonant pad under the shadow sequence (S23); resolves at Plot | "emotional reveal strings slow", "wonder documentary underscore" |
| **M5 "Afterimage"** | 1:15–1:30 | A 3–4-note solo piano motif over strings, ending on an **unresolved** chord that cuts into the loop | "minimal piano mystery ending", "contemplative piano strings unresolved" |

*Practical option:* one well-chosen 2–3-minute "documentary mystery" track can cover M1, M2, M4 and M5 if it has a build and a breakdown. Edit it to these timings and add the M3 silence manually.

## 3. Sound effects

All diegetic sound should be quiet and textural. The VO carries the film.

- **Bed:** continuous wind (S01–S07, S27, S29), so the loop is seamless.
- **Impacts:** sub-boom (S02 end), soft low thumps (S14), bone "clack" (S10).
- **Foley:** stylus in clay (S03), silk (S06), parchment (S08), linen (S11), drawer and pestle (S13), paper and quill (S21, S24), press "thunk" (S26), drawer slide (S29).
- **Fire:** S11, S20 (top half), S23. Keep it subtle; don't use a "campfire ambience" loop with crickets.
- **Movement:** whooshes down (S15) and up (S17), long outward whoosh (S22). Low-passed, never "swooshy".
- **Avoid:** dinosaur roars (we never show a living dinosaur), cartoon stingers, risers into every cut.

## 4. Audio mix

| Element | Target |
|---|---|
| Final mix | **−14 LUFS integrated**, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP |
| VO | Dominant. Light compression (3:1), de-ess; a gentle high-pass at about 80 Hz |
| Music under VO | About 15–18 dB below VO; sidechain-duck it to the VO |
| Music in gaps (S19 excepted) | Rise 4–6 dB in pauses longer than 0.6 s |
| SFX | Felt more than heard; −20 to −28 LUFS short-term |

## 5. Transitions (use only these)

1. **Hard cut.** The default, used about 80% of the time.
2. **Continuous camera move** across shots (S01→S02, S16→S17, S24→S25).
3. **Dust / smoke whip** (S06→S07, S11→S12). Short: 6–8 frames.
4. **Match-cut / match-dissolve** (S15→S16 layer to ground; S23→S24 line-art to engraving; **S29→S01 loop**).
5. **Cut to black** (S18→S19). Used once.

No zoom transitions, glitch effects, light leaks between cuts, spins or flash frames.

## 6. Color grading

**Look:** "excavation at dusk". Earth tones, deep but not crushed shadows, highlights in dusty gold.

| Step | Setting |
|---|---|
| Base | Normalize every source to Rec.709. Match the AI shots to the practical footage first, not the other way round |
| Contrast | Slight S-curve. Lift blacks to about 3–4% and **tint them toward warm umber**, not neutral or teal |
| Highlights | Roll off softly. Push the highlight hue toward **gold (about 40–45° hue)** |
| Saturation | Global −15 to −20%. Desaturate greens and blues heavily, keep ochres and reds |
| Film emulation | A print-film LUT (Kodak 2383-style) at **40–50%** opacity |
| Grain | 35mm grain at about 15–20% (it should be noticed only on a phone at full brightness) |
| Halation | Subtle red-orange glow around practical light sources (torches, lamps) |
| Vignette | Gentle, about −0.3 stop at the corners |
| **Archival plates** | Keep them neutral-warm (paper should look like paper); add a subtle parchment texture under them; *don't* fake age-damage onto real scans |
| **Reveal (S19–S26)** | Slightly cooler and cleaner than the investigation, to signal moving from myth to science; S20's bottom half is the coolest image in the film |
| **Ending (S27–S29)** | The warmth returns but darker; S29 is cool and grey-green (museum), then dissolves back to warm (the loop) |

**Palette reference:** shadows `#1A140F` · midtones `#6B5640` · sandstone `#B89A72` · gold accent `#C9A45C` · off-white text `#F1E8D6`.

## 8. Subtitles (burned-in)

The **`captions.srt`** file in this folder has phrase-level timings, ready to import into Premiere, Resolve, CapCut or Final Cut. Re-time it to the recorded VO.

| Property | Spec |
|---|---|
| Font | **Inter SemiBold** (or Inter Tight SemiBold) |
| Size | 56–60 px at 1080w |
| Colour | Off-white `#F1E8D6`; **one keyword per line in gold `#C9A45C`** (e.g., *Mycenae*, *linen*, *giants*, *dragon*, *mammals*, *broken*, *understanding*, *1677*, *1842*, *holding*) |
| Legibility | Soft shadow: black at 55%, blur 10 px, offset 0. **No** boxes or heavy outlines |
| Chunking | 2–5 words per caption, 1 line preferred, 2 lines max, max about 26 characters per line |
| Position | Centre-aligned, baseline at about **66% of frame height**. Stay above the Shorts UI (bottom 20%) and away from the right-side buttons (right 15%) |
| Animation | A 3-frame fade-in. No pop or bounce |
| Numbers | Use numerals in captions ("3,000", "66 million", "1677") even though the VO says them in words |
| S19 and S20 | Captions on, but when "FINDING ≠ UNDERSTANDING" is on screen, **hide** the subtitle for that phrase so the words don't appear twice |

**Safe zone:** design inside a centred 900 × 1340 px area (from about 230 px to 1570 px vertically). Nothing important goes in the bottom 350 px.

## 9. Export

- H.264 High profile (or H.265), 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, VBR 2-pass, **target bitrate about 16–20 Mbps**, AAC 320 kbps 48 kHz. (A 2160 × 3840 master is worth exporting if the source footage supports it; YouTube serves higher-quality encodes to higher-resolution uploads.)
- Rec.709, full-range flag off (video levels).
- Keep a **textless master** (no burned subtitles or tags) for re-edits and translations.

## 10. Trim plan (if the recorded VO runs long)

Cut in this order, re-checking the runtime after each:

1. Drop the S06 "China, its comets" beat (−1.3 s) and tighten the setup triad to "Egypt recorded its kings. Greece, its gods."
2. Cut "Maybe even a myth." (−1.5 s). The S23 visual still implies it.
3. Shorten the Augustus line to "Augustus collected 'bones of giants.'" (−0.6 s). Keep "so-called" if possible, because it's an accuracy word.
4. Remove the last tail pause (−0.8 s).

**Never cut:** "clearly", "perhaps", "most", "so-called", or the Plot example. Those are what keep the film accurate.
