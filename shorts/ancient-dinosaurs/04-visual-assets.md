# 04 · Visual Assets

Every asset the edit needs, grouped by how to get it. **Check the licence of every individual file before use.** "Public domain" applies to the *original artwork*; a particular photo or scan of it can carry its own licence. Wikimedia Commons, the Biodiversity Heritage Library (BHL) and museum open-access programmes are the first places to look.

## F. Never do this

- ❌ AI-generate an "ancient papyrus", "cave painting" or "tablet" that shows a dinosaur. That would be a **fake artifact presented as real**, which is exactly what this video argues against.
- ❌ Generate legible fake hieroglyphs, cuneiform, Greek or Latin inscriptions. Keep any generated surfaces blank or out of focus.
- ❌ Use the griffin or *Protoceratops* image as if the link were proven.
- ❌ Use real museum photos without credit, or clips that carry a museum's NC licence on a monetized upload.
