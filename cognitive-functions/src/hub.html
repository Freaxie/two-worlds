Four functions, each in two directions.

Thinking and feeling judge. Intuition and sensation perceive. Each one can face inward, toward a private frame, or outward, toward the shared world. That gives eight functions, shown here in their pairs.

Room I · Thinking TiInternal coherence

Builds a private framework and tests every idea for consistency.

 TeExternal effectiveness

Organises the outside world and tests ideas by their results.
Room II · Intuition NiConvergence

Draws many signals together into a single insight.

 NeDivergence

Opens one idea into many possibilities.
Room III · Feeling FiInner values

Weighs choices against a personal sense of what is right.

 FeShared values

Weighs choices by their effect on the people around it.
Room IV · Sensation SiExperience

Reads the present against what has been lived before.

 SePresence

Meets the present moment directly, through the senses.
